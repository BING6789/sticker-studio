# T42 — Platform Review Advisory Preflight

## Core rule
Platform-review screening is advisory. Warn, explain and suggest safer alternatives, but preserve the user's final creative choice.

If the user explicitly keeps the original copy after being informed of likely review risk, continue production unless higher-level safety requirements independently prohibit the request.

## Risk levels
- R0 — no identified review risk.
- R1 — low/uncertain risk.
- R2 — medium risk: warn + alternatives.
- R3 — high risk: prominent warning based on official rules, strong evidence, or known rejection history.

R3 is not an automatic production block.

## Two-pass preflight
1. Copy preflight before copy lock.
2. Final text+visual preflight before packaging.

Check wording, abbreviations, homophones/evasion forms, gestures, props, visual implications and text-image combinations.

## Evidence hierarchy
1. current official platform rules;
2. verified submission/rejection history;
3. reliable creator/platform cases;
4. heuristic judgment.

Never call a heuristic list an official blacklist.

## User choice
Never silently rewrite, delete, sanitize or replace approved copy. Offer alternatives, record the decision, then follow it.
