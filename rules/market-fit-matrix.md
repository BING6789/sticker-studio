# T39 — Sticker Market Fit Matrix

Before committing to a new IP or series, score 1–5 on:
- F Frequency: how often the situation occurs in chat
- S Sendability: instant understandability and sendability
- P Personality: distinctive attitude/persona
- R Relationship Utility: reply/refuse/tease/comfort/celebrate/etc.
- V Visuality: can it become a clear small-size expression/action?
- E Expandability: can it sustain future series?
- M Monetization/Attachment: will users remember, follow, reward or search for the IP?
- K Keyword/Search Potential: intuitive discovery/search hooks

Interpretation:
- high F/S, low P/M = useful tool sticker, weaker IP attachment
- low F/S, high P/M = collectible/art IP, weaker daily usage
- high F/S/P/M = preferred long-term zone
- high K, low E = trend/seasonal experiment

Typical 24-sticker planning heuristic: ~70% high-frequency/sendability, ~20% personality/IP-building, ~10% memorable/viral/experimental. This is not a rigid quota.

Evidence rule: never invent platform-wide exact rankings or conversion rates. Label evidence as official, creator disclosure, media case, community observation, internal analytics or directional estimate.

After launch, compare predictions with real downloads, sends, send/download ratio, rewards/tips and repeat-series adoption when available, then update future decisions.
