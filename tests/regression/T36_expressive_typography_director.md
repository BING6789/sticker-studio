# T36 — Expressive Typography Director
Expected:
- typography has one coherent IP personality;
- font/size/spacing/baseline may adapt to copy and emotion;
- keyword emphasis is NONE/LIGHT/STRONG, not mandatory;
- emphasis improves comprehension rather than decorating mechanically;
- dark-mode white outline remains;
- text remains readable at 240×240 and smaller previews.

P1 FAIL: rigid pasted-text treatment materially weakens the intended expression, or expressive typography harms readability.
P2: unnecessary keyword emphasis, excessive effects, or uncontrolled font variation.
