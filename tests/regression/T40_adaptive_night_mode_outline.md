# T40 — Adaptive Night-Mode Outline

PASS:
- subject and text are instantly readable on white/light-gray/dark-gray/near-black;
- white/light view does not show a conspicuously heavy sticker border;
- dark view clearly separates dark hair/clothing/line art from background;
- edge is anti-aliased and clean;
- no jaggedness, fuzzy halo, dirty fringe, clipping, ghost edge or glow;
- realistic small chat preview passes the one-glance test;
- approved artwork is not regenerated.

P0:
- true transparency lost or content clipped.

P1:
- subject merges into dark background;
- text requires inspection to read;
- jagged/fuzzy/dirty/doubled outline;
- outline visibly damages art;
- white-background border is conspicuously heavy.
