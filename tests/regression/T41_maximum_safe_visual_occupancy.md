# T41 — Maximum Safe Visual Occupancy

PASS:
- main sticker uses available 240×240 area aggressively but safely;
- cover uses the single approved sticker at maximum safe size;
- 100×100 icon is dominated by the approved head/face and signature expression;
- transparent margins are minimized;
- T40 outline is preserved;
- visual center is correct;
- realistic UI side-by-side comparison does not make the asset look noticeably smaller;
- approved art is resized/repositioned, not regenerated.

P1 FAIL:
- unnecessary transparent margins;
- sticker/icon/cover clearly smaller than peers;
- face or action too small in realistic UI;
- geometric centering but poor visual centering;
- clipping of text, character, gesture or night-mode outline.
